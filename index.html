04 — why zine reading works

Pages, not an endless scroll

Natural stopping points

A page has an edge. Scroll has none — so a page is a place your mind can safely pause, without losing your place.

Spatial memory

"It was near the top of the left page" is a real memory cue in a paginated book — and a meaningless one in a scroll that never repeats a layout.

Less cognitive load

A fixed two-page spread is a bounded, predictable unit. Infinite scroll keeps re-rendering that boundary away, which costs attention you'd rather spend on the words.
